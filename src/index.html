Tutorial

Page 1: Parameters & Keyframes

1
Value Slider - Drag to adjust a parameter in real-time. The numeric input next to it accepts exact values. While an animation plays, sliders of animated parameters follow along.
2
Keyframe Button (◇) - Click to add a keyframe for that parameter at the playhead's current position on the timeline. The diamond is highlighted when the parameter is animated, and filled (◆) when a keyframe exists exactly at the playhead.
3
Auto-Key - While playback is paused and the playhead sits on an existing keyframe, moving the slider updates that keyframe's value automatically.
4
Animating a Parameter - Park the playhead, set a value, press ◇. Move the playhead, change the value, press ◇ again. The timeline interpolates smoothly between your keyframes.

Page 2: The Timeline

1
Tracks - Each animated parameter gets its own horizontal track at the bottom of the screen. Keyframes appear as diamonds. A track appears with its first keyframe and disappears when its last keyframe is removed.
2
Playhead & Scrubbing - Drag on the ruler or a track background to scrub. Drag a diamond horizontally to retime it. Double-click a track to insert a keyframe at that point.
3
Keyframe Properties - Click a diamond to select it. The panel on the right edits its time, value and sharpness: 0 gives a smooth curved ease through the keyframe, 1 a sharp linear corner. Delete with the panel button, the Delete key, or right-click.
4
Transport - ⏮ ▶ ⏭ seek and play/pause (hotkeys: comma, Space, period). ⟳ toggles between looping and ping-pong playback. Duration sets the timeline length in seconds.
5
Export - 📷 saves a PNG of the current frame. ⏺ arms video export: the next time you press play, one full timeline cycle is rendered to MP4 (two passes for ping-pong) so the video loops seamlessly.
